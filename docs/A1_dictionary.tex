% APPENDIX A1 — DICTIONARY WORD LISTS (v34)
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This appendix lists the version-34 dictionary used to compute the
\netscore{} measure. Terms are organised by \textbf{semantic
category} (Policy, Inflation, Employment, Residual) and
\textbf{direction} (Hawkish, Dovish). All terms carry equal weight
within the Laplace-smoothed $(H{-}D)/(H{+}D{+}\alpha)$ scoring
formula (Equation~\ref{eq:catscore}); there is no tier or
signal-strength weighting.

Dictionary phrases are written with the custom 44-word stop list
already removed (the same list used during scoring; see
Section~\ref{sec:meth_scoring}). After Porter stemming and
deduplication, 282 unique bigram/trigram terms remain from the 288
raw entries listed below. Eight entries present in version~33 were
pruned in version~34 because they matched fewer than three
documents across the full corpus; these are noted in each section.

\section*{Forward-Looking Sentence Markers}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Sentences are classified as forward-looking if they contain any of
the following patterns (case-insensitive regex matching on raw,
unstemmed text):

\texttt{will, expected, anticipat*, intend, at least until,
as long as, going forward, would be appropriate,
will be appropriate, appropriate to, forthcoming, future,
period ahead, prospect, outlook, balance of risks,
risks to the outlook, likely to, monitor, watchful,
remain alert, stand ready, prepared to, in coming months,
over the medium, subsequent, project, forecast, foresee,
envisage, on track to, trajectory, path of, aim to,
seek to, looking ahead, over the coming, next meeting,
data dependent, meeting by meeting, conditional, contingent,
if warranted, remains to be seen, too early to, should remain,
commit to, in due course, eventually.}
